I am an engineering student at [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/), where I am pursuing the Diplôme d'Ingénieur (Master's level) with a major in Applied Mathematics & Computer Science. Before that, I studied at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) (B.Eng. in Information Engineering and B.A. in French Literature).

My recent work sits where machine learning meets mathematical modeling. As a research intern at [Inria Saclay](https://www.inria.fr/en/inria-saclay-centre), I applied ANaGRAM, an adaptive natural-gradient method based on the Neural Tangent Kernel, to the training of Physics-Informed Neural Networks, and developed an adaptive collocation-point sampling strategy that improved convergence speed and accuracy. I am currently working on a machine-learning benchmark for chaotic three-body dynamics in astrophysics.

I have also worked on quantitative research at Shanghai Luoshu Investment, on volatility modeling for commodity options and relative value in Chinese Treasury cash and futures markets.

I speak Chinese, English and French. Feel free to reach out by email.
